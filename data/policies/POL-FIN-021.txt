Vendor Payments & Software Spend Policy (Effective 2026)

1. Auto-Approval Limits:
- Routine software subscriptions and vendor invoices up to INR 50,000 are eligible for standard processing when an approved purchase order is attached.
- Any vendor invoice, contract renewal, or software purchase above INR 50,000 requires explicit Finance Director or CFO approval before payment release.

2. Restricted Access:
- Bank account routing details, tax withholding schedules, and vendor credit limits are restricted to the finance role.

3. Urgent Exception Rule:
- Even if a request is marked urgent by a department lead, payments exceeding INR 50,000 still require documented human sign-off.
